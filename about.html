About Formula Shelf

Formula Shelf collects the formulas behind everyday calculations and states them carefully, one per page, with definitions, variables, a worked example and a limitation note.

Publisher

This reference library is published by the same publisher as Tools Horizon, the free online calculators website. The connection is disclosed openly: this site is a companion reference, not a third-party review of that or any other service, and it contains no endorsements.

Accuracy

Formulas are stated in their standard textbook forms. Where conventions differ (GPA bands, gallon sizes, tax rules), the page says so and names the convention it uses. Corrections are welcome via the Contact page and are noted on the corrected page.
