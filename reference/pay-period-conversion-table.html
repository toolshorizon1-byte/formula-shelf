Pay Period Conversion Table: Hourly, Weekly, Monthly, Annual

Formula Shelf Reference Desk · Definition page · Reviewed October 2026

Definition

Standard conversion factors between pay periods at a 40-hour week, with a worked table for common rates.

Annual = Hourly × 2,080 · Monthly = Annual ÷ 12 · Weekly = Annual ÷ 52 · Hourly = Annual ÷ 2,080
$15/hr$31,200 / year
$22/hr$45,760 / year
$30/hr$62,400 / year
$45/hr$93,600 / year
$60,000/yr$28.85 / hr · $5,000 / month
$85,000/yr$40.87 / hr · $7,083 / month

Worked example

The 2,080 figure is 40 hours × 52 weeks of paid time. Part-time schedules scale it directly: at 24 hours a week the factor is 1,248 hours a year.

Read this before using it

Conversions show gross pay before tax and deductions, and they assume all 52 weeks are paid. Unpaid leave reduces the annual figure for hourly workers but not for salaried staff with paid leave.
