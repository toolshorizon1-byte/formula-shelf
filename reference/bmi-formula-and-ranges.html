BMI Formula, Ranges, and Unit Conversions (Reference)

Formula Shelf Reference Desk · Definition page · Reviewed October 2026

Definition

The BMI formula in metric and imperial units, the standard adult ranges, and height/weight conversion factors.

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)² · Imperial: BMI = 703 × weight (lb) ÷ height (in)²
UnderweightBelow 18.5
Healthy range18.5 – 24.9
Overweight range25.0 – 29.9
Obesity range30.0 and above

Worked example

Conversions: 1 inch = 2.54 cm; 1 lb = 0.4536 kg. A person of 5 ft 9 in is 69 in = 1.753 m. At 170 lb (77.1 kg), BMI = 77.1 ÷ 1.753² ≈ 25.1 by metric, or 703 × 170 ÷ 69² ≈ 25.1 by imperial — the two routes agree, as they must.

Read this before using it

BMI is a population screening index, not a diagnosis. It does not distinguish muscle from fat and is not applied the same way to children, whose results are read as percentiles.
