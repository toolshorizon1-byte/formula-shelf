The Monthly Loan Payment Formula, Defined and Worked

Formula Shelf Reference Desk · Definition page · Reviewed October 2026

Definition

The standard amortization payment formula, what each variable means, and one fully worked substitution.

M = P × r(1+r)^n ÷ ((1+r)^n − 1)
MMonthly payment
PPrincipal — the amount borrowed
rMonthly interest rate = annual rate ÷ 12, as a decimal
nNumber of monthly payments = years × 12

Worked example

Borrow $18,000 at 6.5% for 4 years. r = 0.065 ÷ 12 = 0.005417. n = 48. (1+r)^n ≈ 1.2965. M = 18,000 × 0.005417 × 1.2965 ÷ 0.2965 ≈ $426.40 a month.

Read this before using it

The formula assumes a fixed rate and equal monthly payments. If your rate is variable, each rate change restarts the calculation on the remaining balance with the remaining term.
