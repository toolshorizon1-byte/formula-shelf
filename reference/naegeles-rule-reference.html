Naegele's Rule: The Due-Date Reference Formula

Formula Shelf Reference Desk · Definition page · Reviewed October 2026

Definition

The 280-day rule stated precisely, its built-in assumptions, and the standard cycle-length adjustment.

Due date = first day of last menstrual period (LMP) + 280 days
Short form+ 7 days, − 3 months, + 1 year, applied to the LMP
Assumed cycle28 days, ovulation on day 14
Cycle adjustmentAdd (cycle length − 28) days for longer or shorter cycles
From conceptionConception date + 266 days, when the conception date is known

Worked example

LMP of 3 March with a 28-day cycle: +7 days = 10 March; −3 months = 10 December (previous year); +1 year = 10 December of the current cycle year. With a 32-day cycle, add 4 more days: 14 December.

Read this before using it

An early ultrasound can supersede the LMP-based date when the two disagree materially. The rule is an estimate: full-term birth spans roughly 37–42 weeks.
