APR vs Interest Rate: A Reference Definition

Formula Shelf Reference Desk · Definition page · Reviewed October 2026

Definition

Precise definitions of interest rate and APR, what each includes, and which one to compare when two loans differ in fees.

Worked example

The interest rate is the yearly cost of borrowing the principal, before fees. The APR (annual percentage rate) folds in most mandatory fees — origination, closing, broker — spread over the term, so it is usually the higher of the two numbers and the fairer comparison between loans with different fee structures.

Read this before using it

Two loans can share an interest rate and have different APRs. Compare APR with APR, and term with term. An APR comparison is still imperfect for loans you expect to repay very early, because upfront fees are spread over a term you did not use.
